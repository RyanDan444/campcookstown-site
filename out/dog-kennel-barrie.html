Straight answers

What people ask before the first visit.

Is someone with the dogs at night?+
The barn is theirs at night, warm in winter and cool in summer, and a counselor is on the property every night of the year.
What about food?+
Bring their usual food and we serve it exactly as you would, one dog at a time so nobody shares. Forgot it? We have food for $50 plus the cost of the bag.
Can you give medication?+
Yes, at no charge. Bring it in the original container with the instructions.
